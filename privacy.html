Synsyma Technologies Incorporated, operating as Synsyma Home Services (“Synsyma”, “we”, “our” or “us”), is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of customers, contractors, service providers and other individuals who interact with our business.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Synsyma collects, uses, stores, protects and shares personal information when individuals access our services, make bookings, request quotations, communicate with us or work with our platform.

This policy applies to information collected through:

  • The Synsyma website, mobile app and digital platforms.
  • WhatsApp and other messaging channels.
  • Telephone calls, emails and text messages.
  • Online booking and quotation forms.
  • Customer service and business operations.
  • Contractor registration and service coordination.

By using Synsyma Home Services, individuals acknowledge this Privacy Policy and the applicable privacy practices described in it.

2. Personal information we collect

Depending on the nature of the interaction, Synsyma may collect the following categories of personal information.

2.1 Customer information

  • Full name.
  • Telephone and mobile numbers.
  • Email address.
  • Residential or service address.
  • Preferred contact method.
  • Booking and service history.
  • Payment and transaction information.

2.2 Property and service information

To provide accurate quotations and coordinate services, Synsyma may collect:

  • Photographs and videos of properties.
  • Descriptions of cleaning or maintenance requirements.
  • Property size and relevant features.
  • Information about the condition of the property.
  • Special instructions relating to the requested service.
  • Access instructions and appointment details.
  • Relevant safety information voluntarily provided by customers.

2.3 Contractor and service provider information

For individuals and companies registering to provide services through Synsyma, we may collect:

  • Full name, business name and contact details.
  • Residential or business address.
  • Service experience, qualifications, insurance and licences.
  • Availability, service areas and service preferences.
  • A government-approved photo ID, and the type of ID provided.
  • Your agreement to a background check and the result of that check.
  • Your agreement that you are responsible for your own tax obligations, with the date and the version of the wording you agreed to.
  • Links to social media pages or a website, if you choose to provide them.
  • Payment information required for compensation.
  • Work history and customer feedback.

ID documents are stored privately and are visible only to the Synsyma team members authorised to review contractor applications. Background checks are conducted only with your consent, only where appropriate and subject to applicable legal requirements. Social media links are optional and are used by our review team only to help assess an application.

2.4 Technical information

Where individuals access our website or digital platforms, we may collect certain technical information, including IP addresses, device information, browser information, website usage and interaction data. If you allow notifications, we also store a device token so that we can send them.

3. How we use personal information

Synsyma collects and uses personal information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  1. 3.1Creating and managing customer accounts and service requests.
  2. 3.2Preparing quotations and estimating the cost of requested services.
  3. 3.3Matching customers with appropriate contractors and service providers.
  4. 3.4Communicating booking confirmations, appointment reminders and service updates.
  5. 3.5Providing customer support and resolving complaints.
  6. 3.6Processing payments, refunds and other financial transactions.
  7. 3.7Verifying contractor identities, qualifications and eligibility, including ID and background checks, where appropriate.
  8. 3.8Maintaining transaction records and business documentation.
  9. 3.9Improving service quality and operational efficiency.
  10. 3.10Preventing fraud, misuse and unauthorized activities.
  11. 3.11Complying with applicable legal, tax and regulatory requirements.

Synsyma will not collect or use personal information for purposes unrelated to the reasons disclosed to individuals, except where permitted or required by law.

4. Sharing of personal information

Synsyma understands the importance of confidentiality and will only share personal information where reasonably necessary for business operations or where legally permitted. Information may be shared with:

4.1 Contractors and service providers

Relevant customer information, including names, service addresses, contact details, photographs and job instructions, may be shared with assigned contractors to enable them to assess and complete the requested work.

4.2 Payment processors

Payment information may be processed through approved third-party payment service providers.

4.3 Technology and business partners

Synsyma may use third-party service providers for website hosting, booking management, communications, notifications, data storage and other operational functions. Such providers may access personal information only as necessary to perform their contracted services, subject to appropriate safeguards.

4.4 Background check providers

Where a contractor has consented to a background check, the information needed to carry it out may be shared with the organisation that performs it.

4.5 Legal and regulatory authorities

Information may be disclosed where required by law, court orders, regulatory obligations or other lawful requests.

4.6 Business transactions

Where Synsyma undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of business assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Synsyma does not sell customer personal information to third parties as a business practice.

5. Photographs and property images

Customers may be required to submit photographs or videos of their properties to enable Synsyma to assess the nature, size and complexity of requested services.

  1. 5.1Photographs and videos will be used primarily for quotation preparation, job assessment and service coordination.
  2. 5.2Relevant images may be shared with assigned contractors for the purpose of preparing for or completing the requested work.
  3. 5.3Synsyma will take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized use, access or disclosure of property images.
  4. 5.4Photographs and videos will not be used in advertising, promotional materials or public marketing without appropriate permission.
  5. 5.5Customers are encouraged to avoid including unnecessary images of individuals, identification documents, valuables or other confidential personal information when submitting property photographs.

6. WhatsApp, email and other communication channels

Synsyma may use WhatsApp, telephone, email, SMS, in-app chat, push notifications and other approved communication channels to manage customer and contractor relationships. These channels may be used for:

  • Booking confirmations.
  • Quotations and service estimates.
  • Appointment reminders.
  • Contractor dispatch notifications.
  • Payment confirmations.
  • Customer support.
  • Service updates and operational announcements.

Where third-party messaging platforms are used, their own privacy policies and security practices may also apply. Customers and contractors should avoid transmitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through ordinary messaging channels.

7. Consent and personal information

Synsyma will obtain consent for the collection, use and disclosure of personal information where required by applicable privacy legislation.

  1. 7.1Consent may be provided electronically, verbally or in writing, depending on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements.
  2. 7.2Individuals will be informed about the purposes for which their personal information is collected.
  3. 7.3Where information is required to deliver a requested service, refusal to provide necessary information may prevent Synsyma from completing the booking.
  4. 7.4Individuals may withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice requirements.
  5. 7.5Withdrawal of consent may affect Synsyma’s ability to continue providing certain services.
  6. 7.6Where Synsyma intends to use personal information for a materially different purpose, additional consent will be obtained where required.

8. Storage, retention and security

Synsyma takes reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, theft, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These measures may include:

  • Restricted access to customer and contractor information, with ID documents available only to authorised personnel.
  • Password-protected business systems.
  • Secure digital storage.
  • Appropriate access controls for personnel and contractors.
  • Confidentiality obligations.
  • Reasonable security practices for business communications.

Personal information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations. When personal information is no longer required, Synsyma will take reasonable steps to securely destroy, erase or anonymize it.

9. Data breaches and unauthorized access

Synsyma will take reasonable steps to prevent and respond to privacy breaches. Where a breach involving personal information occurs, Synsyma will assess the circumstances and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Where notification is legally required, affected individuals and relevant authorities will be notified within the prescribed timeframes.

10. Customer and contractor privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have the right to:

  1. 10.1Request access to personal information held about them by Synsyma.
  2. 10.2Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  3. 10.3Ask questions about how their personal information is being used or disclosed.
  4. 10.4Withdraw consent to certain uses or disclosures.
  5. 10.5Raise concerns or submit complaints regarding Synsyma’s privacy practices.

Requests should be directed to Synsyma using the contact details in this policy. Synsyma will review and respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements. Customers can also delete their account from the Profile tab of the app.

11. Third-party websites and services

Synsyma may use or provide links to third-party websites, payment systems, communication platforms and other external services. Synsyma does not control the privacy policies or security practices of independent third-party platforms. Individuals are encouraged to review the privacy policies of such services before using them.

12. Children and minors

Synsyma Home Services is intended primarily for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for independent service bookings without appropriate parental or guardian involvement and consent where required by law.

13. Changes to this privacy policy

Synsyma reserves the right to revise or update this Privacy Policy as its business operations, technology and legal obligations evolve. Any material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels, including our website or digital platforms. The revised policy will include an updated revision date.
