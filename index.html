    Your data

    Delete your account

    You can delete your Synsyma account yourself, at any time, from the app.

    In the app

    1. Open the Synsyma app and go to the Profile tab.
    2. Tap Delete account.
    3. Choose a reason (optional) and enter your password.
    4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on all devices.

    What happens

    • Your account and sign-in access are removed.
    • Notifications to your phone stop.
    • We keep a record that the account was deleted: your name, email, phone number, the reason you gave, the date, and the platform you used. We keep it for our records and to handle any later questions.

    This cannot be undone. To use Synsyma again you would create a new account.

    Cannot open the app?

    If you can no longer use the app, email us from the address on your account with the subject Delete my account, and we will handle it for you.
